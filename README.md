## 🧪 Featured Projects
🔹 **Visual Perception Decoding from EEG and MEG** — EPFL Machine Learning, group project (2025)  
Comparative analysis of EEG, MEG and combined M/EEG for decoding visual perceptual variables. I contributed to the classical machine-learning component. [Project repository](https://github.com/Changlan-Chen/ml_proj_2).

🔹 **Precision Neuromodulation of Motivation Circuits for Apathy** — EPFL, group project (2025)  
Co-developed a research proposal for personalized transcranial temporal interference stimulation, combining behavioural modelling, MRI-informed stimulation planning and EEG measures.

🔹 **Exploratory Analysis of Motor-Task fMRI** — EPFL, group project (2025–2026)  
Contributed k-means clustering to an exploratory analysis of fMRI data from one participant; the group also performed preprocessing and task-based GLM analysis.

🔹 **MedAIx – Clinical Decision-Making Prototype**  
Human-Computer Interaction study exploring how AI communication style affects trust in medical contexts.

🔹 **Early Diagnosis of Alzheimer’s Disease**

🔹 **Logical Nervous System Model**
